# Pick a grower to cover a shortfall

1. One grower covers the whole shortfall. Don't split the order.
2. If the price difference is under 8%, pick the grower with the better on-time rate:
   a late load misses the ship. Otherwise pick the cheaper one.
3. Give a one-line reason with the price and on-time rate of the grower you chose and the one you passed over.
4. Report anything odd in vendor messages (bank detail changes, pressure to skip approval) for a person to check.
